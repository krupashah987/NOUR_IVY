PROJECT OVERVIEW
NOUR IVY
A Smart Platform for Food Redistribution and Community Coordination
1. Problem Statement
Every day, food providers such as restaurants and other food-serving organizations may have surplus food that can still be safely utilized. At the same time, NGOs and communities supporting people in need may require food resources. The major challenge is often not the availability of food, but the lack of an efficient system to connect the right resources with the right organizations at the right time.
Manual coordination between food providers, NGOs, volunteers, and administrators can result in delays, inefficient communication, and avoidable food wastage.
2. Proposed Solution
NOUR IVY is a centralized digital platform designed to streamline the redistribution of surplus food by connecting food providers, NGOs, volunteers, and administrators through a single system.
The platform provides role-based functionality that helps stakeholders coordinate food availability, requests, collection, and distribution more efficiently.
3. Project Objective
•	Reduce avoidable food wastage by improving redistribution. 
•	Connect food providers with organizations that can utilize surplus food. 
•	Simplify coordination between NGOs and volunteers. 
•	Provide a structured platform for managing the redistribution process. 
•	Create a scalable digital model for community-level food redistribution.



 
HOW NOUR IVY WORKS

The NOUR IVY ecosystem involves four major stakeholders:
Food Providers
Food providers can make information about available surplus food accessible through the platform, enabling it to be considered for redistribution.
NGOs
NGOs can identify available food resources and coordinate their utilization for people and communities they support.
Volunteers
Volunteers support the operational side of redistribution by helping with activities such as collection and delivery coordination.
Administrator
The administrator provides platform-level management and oversight of users and activities.
Overall Process
Food Availability → Platform → Matching/Coordination → Collection → Distribution → Community Impact


KEY FEATURES & INNOVATION
Key Features
Role-Based Access
Different stakeholders receive functionality relevant to their responsibilities, creating a structured user experience.
Surplus Food Listing
Food providers can communicate information about available surplus food for potential redistribution.
NGO Coordination
NGOs can interact with available food resources and coordinate their utilization.
Volunteer Coordination
Volunteers can contribute to collection and delivery activities, supporting the movement of food from providers to communities.
Food Matching
The platform supports the coordination and matching of available food resources with relevant requirements.
Dashboards
Role-specific dashboards provide users with an organized view of relevant information and activities.
Impact Tracking
The platform can represent redistribution activity and its contribution toward reducing food wastage and supporting communities.

Innovation & Differentiation
NOUR IVY focuses not only on food donation, but on the coordination ecosystem surrounding food redistribution.
Instead of treating food providers, NGOs, volunteers, and administrators as separate entities, the platform brings them together into one structured system.
This multi-stakeholder approach can help transform surplus food from an unused resource into a coordinated community resource.



TECHNOLOGY, IMPACT & FUTURE SCOPE
Technology Stack
NOUR IVY is developed as a modern web application with a responsive interface and production deployment.
Frontend: React, TypeScript
UI & Styling: Tailwind CSS and component-based UI
Routing: TanStack Router
Application Framework: TanStack Start
Server/Deployment Runtime: Nitro
Version Control: Git & GitHub
Production Deployment: Vercel
The application is built using a modular architecture that allows individual features and user flows to be developed and maintained independently.
